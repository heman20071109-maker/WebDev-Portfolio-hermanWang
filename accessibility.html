Accessibility Statement

Designing an inclusive web for everyone

1. High-Contrast Theme Engineering

I developed a unique three-way theme toggle. Alongside the standard Light and Dark modes, I specifically engineered a high-contrast Colorblind Mode. This mode utilizes vivid cyan and yellow text against a pure black background to assist users with varying degrees of color vision deficiency.

2. Semantic HTML & Screen Readers

A website must make sense even without its visual styling. I strictly utilized HTML5 semantic tags such as <header>, <nav>, <main>, and <footer>. This structural clarity allows assistive screen readers to accurately narrate the content hierarchy to visually impaired users.

3. Media Alternatives & Scalability

Every single image on this portfolio is accompanied by a descriptive alt text, and video elements utilize aria-label tags to provide context. Furthermore, I implemented a dynamic Font Size Toggle, empowering users to scale the typography to their comfort level without breaking the responsive grid layout.
