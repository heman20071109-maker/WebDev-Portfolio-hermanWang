UN Global Goals

Championing Goal 3: Good Health and Well-being

Physical Health Through Sports

As an avid ice hockey player, I deeply understand the profound impact physical activity has on the human body. Goal 3 emphasizes healthy lives and well-being for all ages. Through my active participation in hockey, I promote cardiovascular endurance, physical fitness, and mental resilience. I strongly advocate for youth sports as a foundation for a sustainable, healthy society.

Mental Well-being in the Digital Space

At first glance, web development and health might seem unrelated, but they are intricately connected. We spend hours staring at monitors. Poorly designed websites with glaring backgrounds and tiny fonts can cause severe eye strain, headaches, and mental fatigue. By engineering a Dark Mode and implementing scalable font sizes, I am actively contributing to digital well-being.

Inclusive Health

Health also encompasses accessibility. My custom Colorblind Mode ensures that visually impaired users do not suffer from digital exclusion, which can negatively impact mental health. Through this portfolio, my ultimate message is that modern technology should be designed to protect, include, and enhance human health.
